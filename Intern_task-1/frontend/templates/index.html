Survival Analysis vs Binary Classification

Traditional classifiers (Logistic Regression, Random Forest classifiers, XGBoost) evaluate attrition as a static snapshot. They discard employee tenure duration and mistakenly treat currently active employees as permanently loyal, introducing severe survival bias.

Ensemble Fusion Architecture

The proposed architecture dynamically blends semi-parametric Cox Proportional Hazards with non-parametric Random Survival Forests. Cox provides a stable statistical anchor while RSF captures high-order feature interactions, resulting in minimal Brier calibration error.

Why Static Binary Classification Falls Short

Standard binary models treat attrition as a boolean flag ($0$ or $1$) at an arbitrary time observation. They discard employee tenure duration and treat censored employees (who haven't left yet) identically to permanently retained staff. In contrast, our survival models predict the continuous probability curve of retention over time.
