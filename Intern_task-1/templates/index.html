Why Static Binary Classification Falls Short

Standard binary classifiers (Logistic Regression, Random Forest classifiers, XGBoost classifiers) treat attrition as an instantaneous boolean snapshot ($0$ or $1$). They completely discard tenure duration and treat censored employees (who haven't left yet) identically to permanently loyal staff. Our survival models capture the entire trajectory of departure probability over time.
